Karatsuba:

karatsuba is a divide and conquer algorithm

T(n/2) represents the time taken to recursively multiply two numbers that are half the size (half the number of digits) of the original n-digit numbers.

3*t(n/2) = o(n^log(2)3) = o(n^1.585)

karatsuba has only 3 multiplication operations

addition = o(n), mult = o(n^2)

faster only above a cutoff

powers of 2:
instead of actual multiplication, it just shifts the bits by using powers of 2
